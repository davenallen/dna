      <p><strong>Role:</strong> Brand identity design</p>
      <h5>The challenge</h5>
      <p>Consonance Productions needed a mark that felt musical without relying on cliché icons, and professional enough for client-facing materials, studio signage, and digital platforms.</p>
      <h5>What I did</h5>
      <ul>
        <li>Developed a minimal interlocking double-“C” symbol that communicates harmony, balance, and collaboration.</li>
        <li>Paired the mark with a clean modern wordmark and spacing system for flexible lockups.</li>
        <li>Designed for monochrome-first versatility so the brand holds up on screens, apparel, and print.</li>
      </ul>
      <h5>Outcome</h5>
      <p>A contemporary studio identity that reads as precise and creative—easy to apply across channels without constant redesign.</p>
      <p><em>Skills demonstrated:</em> Brand systems, logo design, typography, visual hierarchy, production-ready assets (Illustrator).</p>
