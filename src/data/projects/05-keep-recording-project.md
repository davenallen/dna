      <p><strong>Role:</strong> Brand identity design</p>
      <p><strong>Client:</strong> The Keep Recording Studio — Denver, CO</p>
      <h5>The challenge</h5>
      <p>The studio needed an iconic mark that felt analog and musical, but also strong enough for signage, merch, and digital presence. The name “The Keep” invited a protection/preservation metaphor without looking gimmicky.</p>
      <h5>What I did</h5>
      <ul>
        <li>Combined a vinyl-record motif with a minimal castle keep at the center—music + preservation in one symbol.</li>
        <li>Integrated bold typography into the architecture of the mark for a commanding wordmark presence.</li>
        <li>Used high-contrast black-and-white treatment for premium feel and maximum application versatility.</li>
        <li>Designed for multi-channel use: studio branding, albums, merch, and web.</li>
      </ul>
      <h5>Outcome</h5>
      <p>A distinctive local studio brand that balances vintage warmth with contemporary edge—memorable and production-ready.</p>
      <p><em>Skills demonstrated:</em> Conceptual branding, logo systems, typography integration, brand storytelling, asset versatility.</p>
    '
