description:
  title: Case study
  content: |
    <p><strong>Role:</strong> UI design + frontend</p>
    <p><strong>Context:</strong> A lead page that has to qualify someone before it asks for a name. The original was a long quiz with one question on screen at a time.</p>
    <h5>The decision</h5>
    <p>A person should answer the current question and move on, not face the whole form. The contact fields come after the questions. The result stays on the page.</p>
    <h5>What I owned</h5>
    <ul>
      <li>The question list: an age range, four yes or no questions, and a short note.</li>
      <li>The contact step: first name, last name, email, phone, and ZIP, checked before the result.</li>
      <li>The sample result. Form data is kept for demonstration purposes only and will not be sold or shared.</li>
    </ul>
    <h5>How someone else reuses it</h5>
    <p>A new offer passes a list of questions and the contact fields. A choice advances the quiz. The last step is the result, still on the page.</p>
    <h5 id="react">In React</h5>
    <p>LeadQuiz holds the steps. Choosing an answer records lead_step. Reaching the result records lead_complete. Form data is kept for demonstration purposes only and will not be sold or shared. The same idea of keeping a path in the browser is the <a href="/projects/15-disability-benefits/">lead funnel landing page</a>.</p>
